M-SWA
Multi-modal semantic workbench assistant

What it does

M-SWA is a voice-driven workbench assistant. A vision–language model grounds spoken object references; the arm then points at the object with a laser, grasps it and hands it over, while keeping a memory of the objects it has seen.

Physical sensing

Thermal and acoustic sensing let the assistant answer physical questions that vision alone cannot, such as whether a liquid is hot or a motor is overheating or noisy (acoustic instability and thermal-map detection).

Hardware

A Seeed SO-ARM101 Pro leader–follower arm driven by XIAO ESP32-S3 boards.
