Thesis of the paper

For surrogate-driven airfoil design, how the training data are generated matters more than the surrogate architecture. This end-to-end inverse-design pipeline is built around that observation.

Pipeline

  • A physics-regularised Class Shape Transformation (CST) fit of the UIUC airfoil corpus.
  • An NSGA-II data engine that samples across the operating Reynolds-number envelope.
  • A Reynolds-conditioned PointNet++ surrogate trained with a pressure-to-coefficient consistency loss.
  • Final designs verified against SU2 RANS simulations.

Results

The approach collapses the train–test generalisation gap from 9.8× to 1.4× and improves lift-to-drag ratio by 15.4% over a Bézier-GAN baseline.
