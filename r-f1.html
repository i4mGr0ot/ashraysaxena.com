Research · Optimal Control

Joint Optimisation of Active Aerodynamics and Electric Energy Deployment for Minimum Lap Time in 2026 Formula One

Sole-author paper under review at Proceedings of the IMechE, Part D: Journal of Automobile Engineering.

SSRN preprint
Problem
Minimum-time qualifying lap
Controls
Active aero + 350 kW MGU-K
Solver
CasADi / IPOPT collocation
Validation
Within 0.17% of 2026 poles

Motivation

The 2026 Formula One regulations couple two new degrees of freedom: movable aerodynamic elements that switch between high-downforce and low-drag modes, and a 350 kW MGU-K whose deployment is ramped down with speed. Choosing when to switch aero mode and when to deploy or harvest energy are not independent decisions, so this work treats them as a single minimum-time optimal control problem.

Method

  • Direct collocation in CasADi with IPOPT, co-scheduling the active-aero mode with MGU-K deploy and harvest under the regulatory rampdown.
  • A free racing line optimised over the true track limits rather than a fixed reference path.
  • Three vehicle models of increasing fidelity, from a point mass to a transient single-track model.

Outcome

Calibrated to demonstrated capability, the model reproduces real 2026 pole-position lap times to within 0.17%. A preprint is available on SSRN.
