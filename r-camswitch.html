3D-Printed Rotary Cam Switch for Steering-Mode Transitions in a Four-Wheel Independent Steering Rover

V. Shah, A. Saxena, M. S. Dasgupta. Under review at ACDSA 2027 (Int. Conf. on AI, Computer, Data Sciences and Applications).

Vehicle
4WIS steer-by-wire rover
Mechanism
3D-printed rotary cam switch
Modes
Pivot ↔ Ackermann
Testing
Autonomous rover

Problem

A four-wheel independent steering (4WIS) rover can turn on the spot or drive with Ackermann geometry, but switching between these modes must be reliable and verifiable, especially on an autonomous platform.

Approach

A low-cost 3D-printed rotary cam switch paired with an encoder drives and verifies the transition between zero-radius pivot and Ackermann steering. The mechanism was developed for the CRISS Robotics rover and tested on the autonomous vehicle.
