Current Work

Bachelor’s Thesis · Project ATLAS

Credibility Estimation of Future Aircraft Concepts

Institute of Aerospace Systems (ILR), RWTH Aachen University

A method based on Dempster–Shafer evidence theory to judge how far design predictions for aircraft entering service in 2040–45 can be trusted when much of the input data is uncertain or comes from expert judgement. Validated on historical aircraft, then implemented in UNICADO for the kerosene and hydrogen concepts of Project ATLAS, a joint project with Airbus and DLR funded by the German government.

Aug 2026 – Feb 2027 · Aachen · Supervisor: Univ.-Prof. Dr.-Ing. Eike Stumpf · Consultant: Aswin Thagadi Seenu, M.Sc.

How the Elastic Axis Folds the Transonic Flutter Boundary

Transport Phenomena (De Gruyter Brill), accepted · with Dr. Sharad Shrivastava

Shows how moving the elastic axis reshapes the transonic flutter boundary of a pitching and plunging wing section, and explains the resulting fold through energy transfer from shock motion, using SU2 Euler simulations and an actively learned, conformally calibrated surrogate.

AeroelasticitySU2 EulerActive learningConformal UQ

RANS-Verified Surrogate Design of Wing Sections for a uSTOL Aircraft

IAAI-27, accepted · with Dr. Sharad Shrivastava

Trains a surrogate on CFD results to search for wing sections that meet design targets, then checks the final designs with RANS CFD: across a tenfold range of Reynolds number and two turbulence models, predicted drag and lift held within 2.3% and 2.4%. The system supplied the clean-wing section of the flying uSTOL prototype.

3D-Printed Cam Switch for Steering-Mode Transitions in a 4WIS Rover

ACDSA 2027, accepted · V. Shah, A. Saxena, M. S. Dasgupta

A low-cost 3D-printed rotary cam, with an encoder confirming each switch, that lets a four-wheel independently steered rover change reliably between turning on the spot and Ackermann steering; tested on the CRISS autonomous rover.

Rapid Aerodynamic Optimization of Engine Nacelles

Acceleron Aerospace Journal, vol. 7, 2026 · sole author

A fast Python framework for shaping engine nacelles with an in-house viscous–potential flow solver, showing that local reshaping of the nacelle lip can narrow the design space before expensive Navier–Stokes simulations.

A Critical-Layer Instability of Structural-Acoustic Wall Modes in a Two-Flexible-Plate Channel

Journal of Fluid Mechanics, under review · sole author

Identifies a new convective instability of the wall mode in a flexible-walled channel with Poiseuille flow, driven by the critical layer; it grows with wall flexibility and vanishes for rigid walls. Also derives closed-form low-Mach corrections for all coupled modes.

Joint Optimisation of Active Aerodynamics and Electric Energy Deployment in 2026 Formula One

Proc. IMechE Part D, under review · sole author

Finds the fastest 2026 Formula One qualifying lap by optimising the use of movable aerodynamics and electrical energy within the new regulations, by direct collocation in CasADi/IPOPT; once calibrated, predicted lap times match real 2026 pole laps within 0.17%.
